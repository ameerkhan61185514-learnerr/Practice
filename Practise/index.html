Layer by Layer Mastery Kacchi Dum Formula

Anatomy of Kacchi Gosht Biryani

Unlike Lucknowi or Kolkata biryanis where meat and rice are cooked separately and then combined, authentic Hyderabadi biryani marinates RAW lamb with raw spices and layers it directly beneath semi-cooked rice, allowing the mutton juices to steam through the grains.

Top Layer: Saffron Grains

Long-grain aged basmati steeped in Kashmiri saffron milk, mint, and fresh rose petals.

Middle Layer: The Birista Glaze

Caramelized golden onions fried in pure ghee, green cardamoms, and shahi jeera.

Bottom Deg: The Marinated Gosht

Raw baby goat pieces tenderized in raw papaya, sour curd, and crushed 32 spices that cook in their own steam.

Accompanied by authentic Mirchi ka Salan & Burani Raita

✦ The Nizami Legacy ✦

Three Centuries of Gastronomic Royal Splendor

Hyderabadi cuisine was born from the matrimonial alliance of Turkish culinary subtlety with the fiery, pungent spices of Andhra and Telangana. In the royal kitchens of the Asaf Jahi rulers, hundreds of khansamas competed to create dishes that would enchant the Nizam.

Our founder, Ustad Mohammad Ishaq, pledged in 1954 never to dilute the royal recipe ratios. To this day, our biryani handis are made of red terracotta clay from the Musi riverbank, and every pot is blessed with pure Kashmiri saffron.

Ustad Mir Farooq Ishaq

Head Khansama & Master of Dum (3rd Gen)
